# Location pages to remove (spam update clean-up)

Source: Google Search Console export *Performance on Search*, last 16 months, downloaded 2026-10-06.
Compared against every URL generated by `src/pages/locations/[slug].astro` (27 town hubs) and
`src/pages/locations/[location]/[service].astro` (17 towns × 6 services = 102 pages).

## Summary

- **Town hub pages** (`/locations/<town>`): 27 pages, 938 clicks. Healthy except **2 pages with zero impressions**.
- **Town + service pages** (`/locations/<town>/<service>`): 102 templated pages, only **66 clicks in 16 months**.
  - 83 have **0 clicks** (21 of these never got a single impression).
  - 12 have only 1–2 clicks. 7 have 4+ clicks.
- This town × service grid is the pattern the spam update targets (scaled, near-duplicate, doorway-style pages). Most of these pages don't bring in traffic, so removing them costs very little.

## How to remove them safely

1. **301-redirect** each removed `/locations/<town>/<service>` to its hub `/locations/<town>`, and `/locations/galston` and `/locations/dalrymple` to `/locations` (add these to `public/_redirects`). Don't use 404s, because some of these URLs have backlinks or impressions.
2. Remove the slugs from the page generators: `LOW_RANKING_LOCATIONS`/`getLocationServiceStaticPaths` in `src/data/locationServices.ts` (it needs a per-town allow-list of services instead of every combination), and `galston`/`dalrymple` in `src/pages/locations/[slug].astro`.
3. Update internal links: `getServiceLink()` links to the town + service page for every service in the 17 towns. It should fall back to `/services/<service>` for removed combinations. `NEARBY_LOCATIONS` also refers to `galston`.
4. Remove the URLs from `public/sitemap.xml`, then resubmit the sitemap in Search Console.
